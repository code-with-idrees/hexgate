# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Hexgate to be a place where people can ask basic questions, disagree
about design, and ship code without being made to feel unwelcome. That applies
regardless of experience, identity, background, or which editor you use.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake was malicious.
- Give reproductions and evidence, not just "it is broken".
- Respect provider terms of service and rate limits. Do not use the project to
  evade them.
- Accept that maintainers may decline features that make the gateway larger,
  cloud-dependent, or harder to audit.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing someone's private information, logs, or keys.
- Spam, sexually explicit content, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Encouraging abuse of a provider, credential theft, or bypassing paywalls.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space. It
also applies when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content, and may block accounts for
repeated or severe violations. Reports can be sent privately to a maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific policy, informed by the Contributor Covenant.
